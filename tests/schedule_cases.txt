# Schedule texts and what both parsers (parse_schedule() in C++, schedule() +
# _apply_unit() in __init__.py) must make of them. Read by test_logic.cpp and
# test_config.py, so the two can't drift apart.
#
#   unit | schedule text | ok | number of switch points a week
#   unit | schedule text | error

# Valid
C|mon-fri 07:00 eco 18:15 comfort; sat,sun 07:00/17.5 16:00/comfort|ok|14
C|daily 07:00 eco 21:00 comfort|ok|14
C|all 06:00 off|ok|7
C|mon 7:00 20|ok|1
C|fri-mon 08:00 off|ok|4
C|MON 07:00 ECO|ok|1
C|mon 07:00 reduced|ok|1
C|mon 00:00 5 23:59 30|ok|2
C|mon 07:00 20.5; tue 07:00 21|ok|2
C|daily 00:00 20 02:00 21 04:00 20 06:00 21 08:00 20 10:00 21 12:00 20 14:00 21 16:00 20 18:00 21 20:00 20 22:00 21|ok|84
